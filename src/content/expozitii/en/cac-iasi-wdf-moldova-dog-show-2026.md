A **National CAC** show held under the World Dog Federation on Saturday, **5 September 2026**,
at **USV Arena Iași — Agronomy campus**, in partnership with the University of Life Sciences.
Organiser: Carpathians Professional Cynological Association, Collective Member of CFC-Royal.

The show takes place **immediately after the international C.A.C.I.B. show closes**, at the same
venue, and marks the **inauguration of the Carpathians Professional Cynological Association**.

## Entries are closed — participation is automatic

This show **takes no separate entries** and has no entry form of its own.

**Everyone entered in the [C.A.C.I.B. show in Iași](/en/expozitii/iasi-wdf-moldova-dog-show-2026/)
is automatically and free of charge entered in this show as well**, with no additional fee and
nothing further to do.

If you have not entered yet, **[enter the C.A.C.I.B. →](/en/inscriere-expozitie/)** — taking part
in the national show follows on its own.

What this means in practice:

- dogs entered in the C.A.C.I.B. will be **judged twice** on the same day — in the international
  show (CAJC, CAC, CACIB) and in the national one (CAJC, CAC);
- the **starting time and ring order are announced on site**, on the day of the show;
- catalogue numbers for both shows are sent by e-mail when the catalogue closes.

Should you prefer your dog to take part **only** in the international show, simply write to us at
[contact@cfc-royal.ro](mailto:contact@cfc-royal.ro).

On show day, the live ring schedule is available at [cfc-royal.ro/live](/live).
