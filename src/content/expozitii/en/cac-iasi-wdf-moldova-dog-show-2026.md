A **National CAC** show held under the World Dog Federation on Saturday, **5 September 2026**,
at **USV Arena Iași — Agronomy campus**, in partnership with the University of Life Sciences.
Organiser: Carpathians Professional Cynological Association, Collective Member of CFC-Royal.

The show took place **immediately after the international C.A.C.I.B. show closed**, at the same
venue, and marked the **inauguration of the Carpathians Professional Cynological Association**.
Every dog entered in the C.A.C.I.B. took part automatically, at no extra fee, and was judged
twice on the same day.
