An **international C.A.C.I.B.** dog show held under the World Dog Federation on Saturday,
**3 October 2026**, at **Cabana Bolboci** (Moroeni, Dâmbovița County), in partnership with
Cabana Bolboci. Organiser: **Străjerii Munților Association**, Collective Member of CFC-Royal.

## A show dedicated to livestock guardian dogs

The “Bucegi Cup” is a **special** show: only livestock guardian breeds — dogs that guard and
defend flocks of sheep and goats and herds of cattle — are admitted:

- **Romanian shepherd dogs** — Romanian Carpathian, Romanian Mioritic, Romanian Bucovina,
  Romanian Raven (Corb) and Romanian Bălan Shepherd Dogs;
- **Russian shepherd dogs** — Caucasian Shepherd Dog, Central Asian Shepherd Dog, Volkodav,
  Moscow Watchdog;
- **Turkish shepherd dogs** — Kangal, Akbash, Boz, Malakli, Anatolian Shepherd Dog, Karadeniz;
- **any other livestock guardian breed** (for example Tibetan Mastiff, Kuvasz, Komondor,
  Šarplaninac, Tornjak, Karakachan, Karst Shepherd Dog, Maremmano-Abruzzese, Bankhar, Ghadrejani).

The entry form lists only these breeds.

## Entry fees

Entry fees differ at this show:

- **Romanian Bălan Shepherd Dogs** enter **free of charge**;
- **members of the Străjerii Munților Association** pay **50 lei per dog** (flat rate);
- **non-members** pay **150 lei** for the first dog and **75 lei** for each further dog entered
  by the same owner.

Otherwise, the usual rules of CFC-Royal shows apply.
