In the history of an organisation, every international participation strengthens professional relations and builds institutional identity. Today we look back at the participation of **Mihail Cosmin Neagu**, a member of the Panel of Judges of the Royal Federal Canine Club and an international judge accredited by the **World Dog Federation (WDF)**, as a guest judge at the cynological event organised by **Kynologia Polska**, under the coordination of Mrs Beata Pytlewska, in Węgierska Górka (Poland).

The event brought together, in a single day, four cynological competitions of national and international interest:

- International Dog Show – Championship of Romania (CACIB)
- XVII National Show of Small Breeds (up to 45 cm)
- XI National Group I Dog Show
- II Pudliszon Memorial

## The titles awarded

At the *International Dog Show – Championship of Romania (CACIB)*, the judging carried out by the representative of the Royal Federal Canine Club allowed the awarding of the following official titles:

- **Romanian Junior Champion**
- **Romanian Champion**

These titles are recognised and issued through the Royal Federal Canine Club as an organisation affiliated to the **World Dog Federation (WDF)**, an expression of the institutional cooperation between partner organisations and of the trust placed in our system of judging.

## A cooperation that grows stronger

The participation of Romanian judges in international cynological events reflects the development of professional relations between partner organisations and confirms the regard in which Romanian specialists are held within the international cynological community.

We extend our sincere thanks to **Kynologia Polska** and to **Mrs Beata Pytlewska** for the invitation, for the trust placed in us and for the institutional cooperation that contributes to the development of cynological relations between Romania and Poland.

We congratulate Mihail Cosmin Neagu on his work and on the professionalism with which he represented the Royal Federal Canine Club and Romania at this international event.
