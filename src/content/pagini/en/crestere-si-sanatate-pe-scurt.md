*This is a plain-language overview. The binding text is the
[Breeding and Health Regulation](/en/documente/regulament-crestere-sanatate/) (Board of Directors Decision
no. 181/13-08-2026); in case of any discrepancy, the regulation prevails.*

## Rules that apply to all breeds

**Who may be used for breeding** (Art. 5): a dog identified by microchip, holding a certificate of origin
issued by the association or by an organisation whose documents are recognised, and not under a breeding
exclusion measure. A dog with a typicity certificate (type C) may be used for breeding; its puppies, declared
through a DMF, receive at least type B.

**Age** (Art. 6–7):

- the bitch: mated only after **18 months** and not after **8 years**; between 8 and 9 years, once only, with a
  veterinarian's written opinion sent to the registry before mating;
- the dog: after **12 months**; if used before 15 months, the DNA profile is filed before the DMF.

**Rest of the bitch** (Art. 8): at least **10 months** between two whelpings and at most **3 litters in 24 months**.

**Caesarean section** (Art. 9): a bitch that has whelped **twice by caesarean section** may no longer be used
for breeding. The caesarean is declared with the DMF; failing to declare it is a disciplinary offence.

**Inbreeding** (Art. 11–14): matings between close relatives are prohibited or allowed only with declaration,
according to the degrees in the regulation; a frequently used sire is monitored by the registry.

**Puppies** (Art. 23–24): not to be handed over before **8 weeks**; at handover they are microchipped, dewormed,
vaccinated according to age, with a health record completed by the veterinarian; raised in conditions allowing
movement, cleanliness and contact with people.

## Health tests

**Recognised tests** (Art. 15) and **favourable results** (Art. 17):

| Test | Accepted results | Favourable |
|---|---|---|
| Hip dysplasia (HD) | A, B, C, D, E | A, B, C |
| Elbow dysplasia (ED) | 0, 1, 2, 3 | 0, 1 |
| Eye examination | clear, affected | clear |
| DNA profile | filed | filed |
| Breed-specific genetic test | the laboratory's result | assessed only against a breed requirement |

Tests are performed by a veterinarian and proven with the document issued by the veterinarian or the
laboratory, carrying the dog's microchip. They are filed online, in the breeder's area; the registry verifies
and marks them. An unverified test has no effect. Only the test badge (type and result) is shown publicly,
never the document (Art. 16, 20).

**Breeding recommendation** (Art. 19): a dog with at least one verified favourable test and no unfavourable
test receives the public note “Recommended for breeding — CFC-Royal”. The note is not a health guarantee and
does not replace veterinary advice.

## What is mandatory and what is optional, by group

The regulation introduces health requirements **gradually, breed by breed** (Art. 4 and 18): the Board of
Directors may set, by published decision, the tests mandatory before mating for a given breed, with at least
**12 months'** notice. Until such a decision, tests are **optional** and only produce the breeding recommendation.

| WDF group | Tests mandatory before mating (Board decision) | Optional, recognised tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Sheepdogs and cattle dogs | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |
| 2 — Pinschers, schnauzers, molossoids, mountain dogs and Swiss cattle dogs | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |
| 3 — Terriers | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |
| 4 — Bull-type dogs | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |
| 5 — Primitive-type dogs | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |
| 6 — Scent hounds and blood-tracking dogs | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |
| 7 — Pointing dogs | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |
| 8 — Retrievers, flushing dogs and water dogs | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |
| 9 — Companion and toy dogs | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |
| 10 — Sighthounds | none set yet | HD, ED, eye, DNA, breed genetic |

Decisions setting mandatory tests are published on the site and collected in an annex to the regulation; the
table above is updated with them.

## What happens in case of non-compliance

Breaches are examined under the [Disciplinary Procedure](/en/documente/procedura-disciplinara/). The Board may
order a written warning, rejection of the DMF for the litter concerned, suspension of the right to declare litters
(3 months – 2 years), exclusion of a dog from breeding, or suspension or cancellation of the affix (Art. 25).
A measure is taken only after the breeder has been notified in writing and given at least 10 days to reply.
Certificates issued on false data are cancelled; descendants keep their documents (Art. 26).
