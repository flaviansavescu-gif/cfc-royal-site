We've sent you an e-mail with a confirmation link. **The subscription only takes effect
once you click that link** — this is how we make sure the address really is yours and that
nobody subscribed you in your place.

If the message doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your "Spam" or "Promotions"
folder. The link is valid for 48 hours; after that the request deletes itself and the
address is not kept anywhere.

You can withdraw at any time, with a single click, from any message you receive from us.
