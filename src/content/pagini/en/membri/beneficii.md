## Access to the CFC–Royal community
Belonging to an active community of breeders, judges and specialists who share experience, information
and best practices in the cynological field.

## Genealogical documents reserved for members
Members may request the issuance of genealogical documents reserved for them:

- Breeder's Affix;
- Certificates of Origin (Pedigree), type A;
- Certificates of Origin (Pedigree), type B.

Type C Certificates of Origin (Pedigree) may also be requested by non-members, under the conditions
provided by the World Dog Federation / Royal Federal Canine Club regulations.

## The stud book: filed online, no trips and no paperwork
Members whose fee is up to date file the **Mating and Whelping Declaration** straight from their own
account, at [cfc-royal.ro/registru](/registru/):

- the **registration number is issued on the spot** and sent by e-mail — your proof of filing within
  the 90-day deadline no longer depends on anyone else;
- **a single form**, with as many puppy rows as you need, instead of four printed variants: a mismatch
  between the litter statistics and the rows filled in becomes impossible;
- the **stud owner's confirmation** is given through a link sent by e-mail — it works for stud dogs
  from other associations or from abroad, with no account needed;
- the **four-generation pedigree** is recorded in full, and the certificate type follows from the
  data: Type A if all thirty positions carry registration details, Type B if any is missing;
- the **status of your file** is visible at any time in your own space, without having to ask.

## Documents anyone can verify
Every Certificate of Origin carries a **QR code**. A puppy buyer, a judge at the ring entrance or an
association abroad can check on the spot whether the document is genuine, in Romanian or English, at
[cfc-royal.ro/verifica-pedigree](/en/verifica-pedigree/).

Registered dogs also have a **public record** in the [stud book](/en/caine/): the four-generation
pedigree drawn out, littermates, progeny and show titles. Searching is done by certificate serial
number, WDF number or microchip — not by name, because names repeat across kennels while a microchip
does not.

The breeder's name and affix are shown: breeding is a public, assumed activity. Owner details are
not published.

For a breeder this means something very concrete: **your work can be proven at any time, by anyone**,
without depending on a file forgotten in a drawer.

## Training through the Judging School
Members have access to the programmes of the [Centre for Cynological
Competencies](/en/educatie/centrul-competentelor-chinologice/), with its own study platform:

- course modules, an individual study manual and tests, tracked on a personal account;
- judging exercises, a comparative analysis room and photo-based anatomy tools;
- ring placements recorded and tied directly to the Club's shows;
- examination, certification by WDF group and entry in the public register of judges;
- annual continuing education for judges already certified.

The path from candidate to certified judge is written down, not passed on by word of mouth: every
stage has clear conditions, the same for everyone.

## Shows run with the Exhibition Manager
The Club's shows are organised with our [own management system](/en/manager-expozitii/), which changes
things every exhibitor feels:

- **online entry**, with the age class checked as you fill the form;
- catalogue and ring sheets generated from the same data, so they cannot disagree;
- a **live board** in the hall, showing when each breed is expected in the ring;
- results published on the website right after the event, and diplomas sent by e-mail;
- the title history of every dog, kept from one edition to the next.

## An association that builds its own tools
The association's systems — the stud book, the school, the exhibition manager and the registry office — are not off-the-shelf
software. They are built by the association, for the real work of breeders, judges and organisers,
and they are connected: a title won in the ring reaches the dog's record in the stud book; a ring
placement is recorded in the candidate's file; an issued certificate can be verified from anywhere,
with a phone.

That is what a modern organisation actually means: not that it has a handsome website, but that
**every document it issues can be verified**, every rule is the same for everyone, and members' data
is looked after — encrypted backups, access by role, and nothing on display that has no business
being there.

## Preferential rates
Members benefit from reduced fees for:

- entering shows organised by the Club;
- attending courses, seminars and training programmes;
- products and services offered by the Royal Federal Canine Club or its partners.

## Priority within the organisation's projects
Where certain programmes, services or opportunities involve a limited number of places or selection
criteria, CFC–Royal members are given priority.

## Promotion of cynological activity
The Club promotes the results achieved by its members in competitions, the activity of registered
kennels and the projects that contribute to the development of cynology.

## Involvement in the organisation's development
Members may take part in the Club's projects, put forward proposals and contribute to the development
of the regulations, programmes and initiatives carried out under the aegis of the Royal Federal
Canine Club.

---

Membership requires the payment of an [annual fee](/en/membri/cotizatii/) of **100 RON**, set by
decision of the Board of Directors.
