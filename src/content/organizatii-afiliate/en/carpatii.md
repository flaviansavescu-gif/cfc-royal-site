The Carpații Professional Cynological Association (AChP-Carpații) is a professional cynological
association and a Collective Member of the Royal Federal Canine Club, active in Iași County.

## Shows organised

The association opened its activity on **5 September 2026**, at **USV Arena Iași — Agronomy
campus**, with two shows held the same day under the World Dog Federation:

- **C.A.C.I.B. Iași — WDF Moldova Dog Show, 1st Edition** (international show) —
