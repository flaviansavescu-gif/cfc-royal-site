The **Judging School** of the Centre for the Development of Cynological Competencies trains the
new generation of cynological judges, in line with **World Dog Federation (WDF)** standards. The
2026 series brings together the candidates admitted after the interviews, with training delivered
by the WDF-licensed judges of the Panel of Judges.

## Course platform

The courses take place on the Judging School's dedicated platform: modules open as they are
taught, and candidates take a verification test at the end of each one. Access requires the code
provided by the secretariat.
