The Statute is the **founding act** of the Association Club Federal
Chinologic – Royal: from it flow the association's purpose, membership, governing
bodies and every other regulation published on this site. It was adopted by free
agreement of the **constitutive General Assembly of 15 August 2023**, in Râmnicu Vâlcea.

The official document exists **in Romanian only** — the language in which it was adopted
and filed with the court. The download below is the Romanian original; the summary on
this page tells you what it contains.

## Why some lines are covered

The version published here is the **public** one. The founding members' personal data —
national identification numbers, home addresses, identity card series and numbers — and
their handwritten signatures have been removed. They belong in the copy filed with the
court, where the law requires them; on a public page they would help no one and could
help someone with bad intentions. **The founding members' names and offices remain** —
they are public in any case, in the national register of associations and foundations.

The redaction is applied to the scanned image itself, not over a text layer: there is
nothing underneath the bars to recover.

## Amending the Statute

Under **Art. 46**, the Statute and the constitutive act may be amended
only by decision of the General Assembly, on a proposal of the Board of Directors. Any
future amendment will be published here, alongside this text.

The association's other documents — the Code of Ethics, the Disciplinary Procedure, the
judging and breeding regulations — flow from this Statute and can be found in the
[Documents section](/en/documente/); the record of their adoption is in
[Institutional transparency](/en/organizatia/transparenta/).
