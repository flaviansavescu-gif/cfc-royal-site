The request for reserving a breeder's affix (the kennel name) **is submitted online**,
from your [member area](/registru/) in the Studbook, in accordance with the
[Kennel registration regulations](/en/documente/regulament-inregistrare-canise/).

## How to request your kennel's registration

1. **Enter the register** with your member code (it starts with `MBR-`), at
   [cfc-royal.ro/registru](/registru/). No code yet? You receive one after your
   membership is verified — request it from the register page itself.
2. **Propose up to three affix variants, in order of preference** — as the regulations
   require. Only the first variant is mandatory.
3. **The registry office checks the uniqueness** of each variant against the affixes
   already registered and decides. **The answer comes by e-mail**, and the state of your
   request can be seen at any time in your member area.

After approval, the affix enters your member record: it is filled in automatically on
mating and whelping declarations and appears on the certificates of the kennel's dogs.

---

_The paper form was withdrawn when online submission opened. To change an already
registered affix, write to the Association at contact@cfc-royal.ro._
