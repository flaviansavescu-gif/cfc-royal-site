The mating and whelping declaration **is submitted online**, in the Association's
[Studbook](/registru/), in accordance with the
[Studbook Regulations](/en/documente/regulamentul-registrului-genealogic/) (Art. 4–5).

## How to submit the declaration

1. **Enter the register** with your member code (it starts with `MBR-`), at
   [cfc-royal.ro/registru](/registru/). No code yet? You receive one after your membership
   is verified — request it from the register page itself.
2. **Fill in the declaration online**: the parents' details, the litter and the puppies,
   with the parents' pedigree documents attached (photographed or scanned — large scans
   are accepted too).
3. **Follow your file's progress** directly in the register: you can see at any moment
   where it stands and what is still missing.

The submission deadline is **90 days from whelping**, and the certificates are issued
within at most **15 days** of the complete file and the confirmed mating.

## The only paper that remains

The mating is confirmed by the owner of the male, through a link received by e-mail. Only
where this route is not possible does the registry office accept instead the **signed paper
proof of the mating**, which is placed in the file (Regulations, Art. 6 para. 3).

---

_The paper declaration forms were withdrawn when the electronic register came into
operation. A declaration submitted other than online can no longer be accepted._
